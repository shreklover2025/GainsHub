Your GainsHub guide

From your first set to a saved workout.

1. Check your account

Check the “Logging as” label before you start. Each signed-in person has a separate workout history. Sign in under Cloud connection to sync between devices.

Guest workouts stay on this device. To move them to an account, export a backup as Guest, sign in, then import it from History.

2. Choose a workout

From Home, choose Upper Body, Lower — Squat Focus, or Lower — Deadlift Focus. Check the date and add any session notes.

Use last workout’s sets copies the previous weights and reps for that routine. Copied sets start unchecked.

3. Log each set

  1. Enter the weight in pounds and the reps you completed.
  2. Check the set’s box when you finish. This also starts your selected rest timer.
  3. Use + Add set for another set, or × to remove an unused one.

Reference and LAST LOGGED show your own previous saved workout for that exercise. If you have no saved history yet, the app says so. Assisted pull-ups use the assistance amount; dips use 0 for bodyweight or the added load.

4. Finish or skip an exercise

After checking every set for an exercise, select Done to collapse its card. Reopen lets you review or change it.

Select Skip today for an exercise you are leaving out. It collapses and its sets are excluded from the saved workout. Reopen reverses the skip and keeps your entered sets.

Done and Skip today do not save a workout to History.

5. Finish & Save Workout

The finish bar shows how many exercises are done, skipped, or still remaining. Select Finish & Save Workout to put the session in History. Only checked sets with valid weight and reps are saved; at least one completed set is required.

If exercises are unfinished, you can confirm saving only the completed sets. After saving, look for the confirmation. Any new personal records are highlighted against your earlier loaded history.

Without saving, your unfinished draft stays on this device. It can be restored after reopening, but does not sync or update LAST LOGGED.

Review your progress

History lets you review, edit, or delete saved sessions. Progress shows exercise charts and next-session suggestions. Standing and seated calf raises both use a 15-rep target; other exercises default to 8 reps. Suggestions require at least three recorded sets at one weight and never change your workout automatically.

Personal records track higher weight or more reps at the same weight. For assisted pull-ups, less assistance counts as an improvement.

Sync, backups, and offline use

Signed-in saves sync automatically when connected. Check Cloud connection to confirm syncing finished; saving on this device does not guarantee an upload has completed. Sync now lets you retry manually.

If two devices edit the same workout, the app preserves conflicting edits as a separate workout. Review History before deleting any duplicates.

Visit online and wait for “Ready to reopen offline on this device” before relying on offline access. Offline saves upload when your account and connection are available again. First-time sign-in needs a connection.

Export a JSON backup from History regularly. Import backup adds new workout IDs and keeps existing IDs unchanged. Clearing browser storage can erase drafts and unsynced workouts.

Install and update

Where supported, use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home Screen option. You can also keep using GainsHub in your browser.

When Update available appears, select Update now. The app saves your unfinished draft before reloading. If it reports a storage error, keep the page open and export a backup first.
